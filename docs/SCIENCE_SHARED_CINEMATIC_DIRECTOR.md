# Visual milestone: moving science cameras and sculpted volcanic terrain

The original `volcano` and `aurora` engines share an automatic
per-event cinematic camera (smooth position, focal plane and uniform-scale
zoom). A volcano chamber shot is genuinely close, the magma-ascent shot
tracks upward, eruption follows the plume, and lava returns to a wide reveal.
The same director varies five Northern Lights camera trajectories.

The volcanic terrain now uses cached asymmetric shadow facets, fractured
rock seams and sculpted crater lighting. Lava rivers follow detailed
spatially stable sinuous paths, with independently advancing molten fronts,
moving incandescent tracers, dark cooling crust, orange local light spill
and bright leading caps. These are continuously generated original
animated surfaces, not recycled static background illustrations.
The entire work remains original CPU-only Pillow/Python without paid models.

The content cache version for portrait science has changed to v3; previously
encoded science clips cannot bypass the updated camera/materials. Audio,
captions, clip durations, narration, soundtrack and editorial safe areas stay
unaltered. Scientific visuals are still schematic, not calibrated physics.

# How Do Volcanoes Erupt? (original English video)

Five causal 1080x1920 science scenes, composed from deterministic Pillow art:
1. Melting rocks and an underground magma reservoir (schematic cutaway).
2. Pressure increasing in trapped gas bubbles and magma chamber.
3. Molten rock rising continuously along the vertical conduit.
4. Plume of ash and independent ballistic glowing ejecta.
5. Lava streams with moving tips and layered incandescent flow surfaces.

Every shot is a moving 1080x1380 field centered in the native portrait
video. The header, chapter progress indicator and captions are drawn
after the animation so they cannot drift or overlap on Android. Natural
English Kokoro, original procedural music, audio-boundary subtitles and
full H.264/AAC checking are provided by the existing release workflow.

These are **schematic geological explanations**, not measured eruption
models or historical footage. No paid model, copyrighted image or paid API.
GitHub Actions produces an UNREVIEWED preview, not a YouTube upload. For
a stable Android URL, the main-branch successful render is SHA-verified,
fully decoded and attached to a GitHub prerelease as volcano-eruption.mp4.

# Original episode: Why Do Northern Lights Dance?

An original English 9:16 narrated science episode created from scratch in
Automate-ytb, with five different procedural 2.5D atmospheric and solar
mechanisms: Sun and solar wind; guiding magnetic field; energetic atmospheric
collisions; energy release by excited gas particles; moving auroral curtains.

* All drawings are made locally by Pillow with deterministic continuous motion.
* Night mountains, solar particles, shaded globes and smoothly moving volumetric
  auroral ribbons are drawn from mathematics, not external video or images.
* English Kokoro af_heart voice, original ambient soundtrack, audio-based phrase
  subtitles, 1080x1920 at 30 fps.
* No external video-provider costs or key; CPU GitHub Actions does the work.
* This is **qualitative explanatory art**, not exact magnetospheric simulation.
* The real MP4, PCM length, decoded frames, timing and subtitles are verified by
  the shared one-click QA before any GitHub prerelease link is published.
* Unreviewed and **never** automatically uploaded to YouTube.

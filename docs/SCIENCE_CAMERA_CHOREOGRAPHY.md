# Event-paced science cinematography

The existing original 1080×1380 domain animation stays unchanged. The
volcano and aurora camera now follows authored action-phase knots instead of
one generic smoothstep per shot. The camera holds its subject briefly,
travels during the causal event and eases into the visual payoff. Each
segment uses C1-continuous interpolation, bounded cropping and the existing
uniform Pillow affine transform. Fixed portrait captions remain unaffected.

No extra frames, model inference, paid assets, or new dependencies. The
existing source-space zoom/framing endpoints and 30 FPS encoder remain
unchanged. This is visual choreography, not photorealism or audio-phoneme
alignment. A real rendered MP4 and human editorial review are still required
before claiming cinematic approval.

## Editorial truthfulness

These source clips document **real jellyfish**, not proof that the pictured
individuals are emitting light. Any added bioluminescent pulses, particles or
enhanced colors are an **artistic explanation**. Do not claim a real recorded
reaction or identify a specific species unless externally verified.

Suggested description disclosure:
"Real jellyfish filmed by NOAA Ocean Exploration (public domain).
Editorial color grading and vertical reframing; voice-over explains how
bioluminescence can work, but these clips do not verify light production.
Music generated originally; Kokoro neural English narration."

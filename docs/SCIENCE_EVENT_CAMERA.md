# Event-directed cinematic camera (science shorts)

The camera director now uses deterministic, compact action envelopes keyed
to the authored scientific beat. Volcano pressure builds toward an emphasis,
eruption responds to the vent burst, ascent follows the rising material,
and aurora collisions/emission get their own gentle focal response.

Each emphasis is a small temporary zoom and source-space shift over the
existing continuous camera path. The envelope returns to zero before both
scene boundaries; subtitles and UI remain stationary and safe. Source-space
crops remain bounded. No shake, randomized cuts, stock video or paid model.

This improves camera direction rather than changing physical scene content.
Full-resolution preview and mobile readability still require real render QA.

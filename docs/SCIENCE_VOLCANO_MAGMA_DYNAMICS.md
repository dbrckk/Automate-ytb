# Premium volcano cutaway: causal magma animation

The first three clips of the original **How Do Volcanoes Erupt?**
film now use one original underground motion engine.

- **Magma:** a circulating molten reservoir with moving cellular hot spots,
  curved glowing convection traces and chamber-clipped textures.
- **Pressure:** many rising gas bubbles within the chamber, expanding
  pressure waves and progressively growing wall fractures outside the magma
  rather than arbitrary lightning in the sky.
- **Ascent:** a continuous monotonic molten front advancing up the rock
  conduit, with bright internal flow and branching wall cracks that
  activate as the front rises.

The chamber ellipse and vent geometry are pedagogical schematic cutaways,
not actual CFD or calibrated geological measurements. Deterministic
CPU rendering uses cached 1080x1380 matte masks, with glow at 270x345.
No external copyrighted imagery or paid render services.

This integrates with the existing volcanic 3D heightfield, projected lava
light, ballistic eruption ash, English Kokoro narration and safe
mobile typography. Technical gates are the existing full H264/AAC MP4
workflow and the shared CI suite; editorial artistic review stays separate.

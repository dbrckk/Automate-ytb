# P5.27 — Physically causal original 2.5D narrative shots

**Actual defect:** The independently decoded 2026-11-10 *Rainbow Chase* MP4
showed a stationary stick-figure observer and a stationary schematic arch
although the final narration said, “when you move, its apparent end moves
too”. The three middle shots also recycled nearly the same round droplet
framing, while photon tracers travelled along always fully drawn paths.

## New reusable physical source-action contract

- `automate_ytb/causal_action25d.py` exposes a shared validated,
  continuously differentiable event clock. Speech-derived phrases control the
  action without claiming exact phoneme/word timestamps. The older ocean
  `event_phase` now calls the same engine, preserving its historical timing;
  the same engine powers the rainbow's optical scene changes.
- `contributing_sources` blends *fixed world-space* nodes as the observer
  moves. No droplet physically follows an observer, no sudden source swap.
  This is qualitative explanatory projection, **not simulated real-world
  geometrical or wave optics**.
- The rainbow chapter's final viewer walks (articulated limbs and coat);
  the colored light paths change their **original source nodes** with
  the moving eye, while a field of original stationary raindrops persists.
  The view-relative bow follows the observer's line of sight; it is not a
  touchable or collectible object placed in the landscape.
- Three earlier refraction/reflection/emergence shots now have different
  **source-space layouts** (scale, spatial lens position, angle of colored
  exit beam). Ray endpoints visibly grow *after* contact with water and
  internal reflection, instead of showing complete cause/effect paths at
  frame 1. Source glass materials and continuous optical tracer effects
  remain reusable, not hand-inserted stock video.
- `science_audio_cues.py` now derives the final viewer's actual movement
  marker from the *synthesized phrase mentioning move/walk*, not from
  arbitrary scene index. The native `PortraitScienceScene` passes that
  cue to the material renderer, without moving title and captions.

**Physical references:** [NOAA/NESDIS explanation of observer and exiting
wavelengths](https://www.nesdis.noaa.gov/about/k-12-education/optical-phenomena/what-causes-rainbow),
[Royal Meteorological Society: a rainbow is specific to observer and
sun/drop geometry](https://www.rmets.org/metmatters/how-are-rainbows-formed),
[US National Weather Service: the rainbow is an angular phenomenon](https://www.weather.gov/fgz/Rainbow).

## Proof / acceptance

- Source tests `tests/test_causal_observer25d.py` verify C1-safe event
  timeline, zero source teleporting, real walker x trajectory (>220 source
  px), source provenance and color-ray crossfade, two unrelated **ocean**
  and **optics** domains, speech-derived trigger and unchanged subtitle
  regions at real 1080×1920 resolution.
- `scripts/review_causal_observer25d.py` runs **both real repository
  checkouts** (latest base main and branch). It renders identical narration,
  topic, scene index, camera time and 1080×1920 resolution for *all five*
  beats. It saves full-resolution originals, contact gallery, changed-pixel
  counts and cryptographic SHA-256 proofs. It fails if any of the four
  altered beats has no material source visual difference, or if mobile
  title/subtitle regions change.
- The existing `rainbow-chase-original-short.yml` workflow is triggered
  after merge and requires a real English Kokoro H.264/AAC 30fps narrated
  MP4, authored soundtrack, caption/media/frozen-frame QA and durable
  byte-verified GitHub Release with history. **Full video not approved
  without a successful run**.

### Non-goals / known remaining limitations

This update does not create physically exact ray tracing, human-motion
capture, 3D PBR, universal photorealistic actors or word-for-word forced
alignment; it is CPU-friendly original explanatory **2.5D** source art.
Chapter opening editorial hook, fine optical surfaces, true camera-track
parallax beyond our existing shared motion engine, neural vocal acting
and subtitles alignment are separate P5.28/P5.29 tasks.

Manual editorial/scientific review is required before publication. No
external paid API, licensed third-party photos, placeholder clips or
automatic TikTok/YouTube uploads.

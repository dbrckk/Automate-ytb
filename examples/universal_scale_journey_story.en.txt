Imagine a luminous thread, only 1 mm long, resting beside a tiny ruler.

Stretch that same glowing thread until it reaches 1 cm. A human silhouette now gives the viewer a sense of scale.

The thread grows to 1 m, while a young tree appears beside the measuring line.

Extend it again to 100 m and a distant tower comes into view beneath the rising light.

At 1 km, mountain peaks enter the landscape, and the measuring line keeps climbing.

Finally the length reaches 100 km. Earth appears as an illustrative backdrop for this mathematical thought experiment.

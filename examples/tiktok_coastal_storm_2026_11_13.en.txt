Imagine a train rushing along a railway at the edge of the sea. Dark storm clouds gather overhead.

Rain waters the forest trees beside the tracks. Their leaves sway as the coastal storm moves closer.

Beyond the railway, an ocean wave rises beside the passing train. Its luminous crest curls toward the shore.

Suddenly, an ocean wave crashes into the train. The locomotive jolts and sea spray explodes across the coastline.

The ocean wave retreats as the train continues along the coastal railway. This is an imagined storm, but the force of water feels unmistakable.
